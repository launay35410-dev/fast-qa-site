FAST · Support

QA training, enablement and support to make quality understandable and sustainable.

Why FAST · Support ?

Even when QA is understood ( FAST · Frame ) and practices are aligned ( FAST · Align ), one challenge remains: knowledge transfer over time.

Teams evolve.
Projects change.
Technical contexts are renewed.

Without a conscious effort to transmit practices, quality gradually degrades and becomes dependent on individuals rather than facts.

FAST · Support aims to anchor QA sustainably by supporting teams and transmitting clear, field-adapted reference points.

What FAST · Support covers

  • Long-term support for QA and product teams
  • Transmission of existing QA practices and principles
  • Skill development on readability and traceability
  • Methodological QA support without tool dependency

FAST · Support is not limited to isolated sessions. It follows a continuity-driven approach.

Training within FAST · Support

Training is a key lever of FAST · Support. It focuses on transmitting durable reference points, not isolated tools or recipes.

  • QA fundamentals and requirements structuring
  • Test strategy, governance and monitoring
  • Automated testing principles, benefits and limits
  • Selenium, Cypress, Playwright
  • ATDD, BDD, TDD, Three Amigos
  • FAST principles and factual traceability

Content is adapted to the real context and maturity level of teams.

What you get

  • More autonomous and QA-aware teams
  • Shared understanding of practices and objectives
  • More robust QA, less dependent on individuals
  • Smoother knowledge transfer during team changes

FAST-QA positioning

FAST · Support continues FAST · Frame and FAST · Align. It consolidates practices through transmission and prepares meaningful use of the FAST framework.

No tools are imposed. Enablement adapts to the real context.
